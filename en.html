Doctrine · founding manifesto

The engineering of cognitive systems.

Applied research position — Synchronia Labs, Algiers.

The problem isn't intelligence. It's integration.

The past decade produced remarkable models — but isolated ones. Each perceives, or reasons, or speaks, or decides. None lives in an environment, with lasting memory and acts that commit. The real obstacle for organizations is no longer a model's performance: it's the absence of an architecture that holds perception, reasoning, memory, decision and action together.

A model is a part. A system is an organism.

We study the system, not the model.

Synchronia Labs does not seek to train a better foundation model — that is the field of players with vast compute. Our research object is cognitive architecture: how to orchestrate existing models, add persistent memory and structured reasoning, and turn them into systems that perceive, decide and learn reliably, traceably and reproducibly.

Sovereignty is a design constraint, not an option.

An intelligent system that depends on a remote API is not under control: it can vanish, change price, or expose data. We design for local execution — on the organization's hardware, its data within its walls. Far from a handicap, this constraint forces a more rigorous architecture and directly serves institutions for whom sovereignty is non-negotiable.

Proof precedes promise.

A cognitive system that acts must be verifiable by the one subject to it. Our work makes traceability the first principle: signed agents, replayable decisions, cryptographic proof chains. We don't ask for trust — we make it controllable. And we display the real state of each system: what runs, what is in progress, what remains a hypothesis.

What cannot be verified is not a result.

An architecture must learn to last.

A frozen system becomes obsolete. We design evolving architectures — that observe their environment, memorize, adjust and improve their decisions over time. Memory is not a cache: it's the organ that turns repeated execution into acquired skill.

What we do not claim

  • We don't build a competing foundation model — we orchestrate the existing.
  • Our perception and robotics systems (vision, swarms, IoT, digital twin) are research programs in development, not finished products. We say so.
  • To date, Synchronia Labs is carried by a single architect. A strength for coherence, a limit for scale — and the reason for this call to collaborate.
  • Our deployed results are real but still few. We prefer four verifiable proofs to forty announcements.

Research programme · Synchronia Labs

AnaHybrid — the sovereign cognitive engine.

Persistent memory · hybrid reasoning · local execution

Operational prototype — evolving

Most AI systems forget. With every query, the model starts from scratch. AnaHybrid explores the opposite hypothesis: a local intelligence with a memory that lasts, that consolidates experience into knowledge, and reasons over what it has lived — without ever depending on the cloud.

The hypothesis

A cognitive architecture with tri-layer memory, running on local hardware, can reach a continuity and contextual relevance that a memoryless model cannot — while keeping data sovereign.

The architecture

Episodic
What happened. A timestamped memory of events and interactions — the raw trace of experience.
Semantic
What it means. Vector search (FAISS): facts linked by meaning, not chronological order.
Conceptual
What we keep. Abstraction: regularities extracted from experience become reusable concepts.

A vertical flow links the layers: the episodic consolidates into the semantic, the semantic distills into the conceptual — the mechanism by which repeated execution becomes a skill.

What's proven

  • The engine runs locally, on GPU, with no cloud call.
  • The three memory layers are implemented and queried.
  • Operational semantic search (FAISS + structured storage).
  • Memory-first routing: the system consults its history before answering.

What's still open

  • Automatic episodic → conceptual consolidation at scale.
  • Quantitative evaluation on reproducible benchmarks.
  • Scaling up: more context, more memory, more compute.
  • Multi-domain and multi-agent generalization.

Why this programme seeks partners

The prototype works — on modest hardware. Its full potential (long memory, deep consolidation, rigorous evaluation) demands a step up in compute that an isolated workstation can't provide. That's exactly the point of the collaboration sought: turning a running hypothesis into a measured, publishable result.

Platform · Synchronia Trust

The QR isn't an image.
It's a proof.

Trust shouldn't depend on a person — it should be guaranteed by the system. Synchronia Trust is the infrastructure that lets a business, a shop or an administration know, with certainty and without relying on anyone's word: who, what, when, where, why, and validated by whom.

Don't just scan a QR code. Verify a truth.

The problem

A receipt can be forged. A ticket copied. A document imitated. Today, nothing proves to whoever receives a paper that it's authentic — they trust, or they don't. Synchronia Trust replaces that blind trust with a checkable proof: a cryptographically signed code anyone can verify in a single scan.
